Admission Rate - PM

Formula:
COUNT(IF MONTH([Date])=DATEPART('month',[Parameters].[Month])-1 AND YEAR([Date])=DATEPART('year',[Parameters].[Month]) AND [Patient Admin Flag] = TRUE THEN [Patient Id] END)/ COUNT(IF MONTH([Date])=DATEPART('month',[Parameters].[Month])-1 AND YEAR([Date])=DATEPART('year',[Parameters].[Month]) THEN [Patient Id] END)
Depends on:
  • Date
  • Parameters
  • Month
  • Patient Admin Flag
  • Patient Id
Used by:
  • Admission Rate - % change
